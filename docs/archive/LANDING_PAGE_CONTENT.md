## Section: FAQs

**Heading**

Questions, _answered._

### What does vetted mean?

A cleaner is called vetted only once SIZA has confirmed the ID matches the submitted selfie, the ID is readable, and both supplied references have been phoned.

### How does payment work?

At launch, payment is arranged directly between you and the cleaner after the job. SIZA does not hold funds or cover payment disputes.

### Do cleaners set their own rates?

Yes. Cleaners set their own rates and can describe whether their rate is per hour or per job in their application.

### Where is SIZA available?

SIZA is launching in Johannesburg and is onboarding its first cleaners. Availability depends on the suburbs and services each approved cleaner provides.

### What documents do I need to apply?

Have your profile photo, selfie holding your ID, ID document or passport, proof of address, and two references ready before you start.

### How are hosts matched with cleaners?

Hosts share their suburb, property details, service and preferred time. SIZA reviews the request and assigns an approved cleaner serving that area and service.

## Copy and claims guardrails

- Use “vetted” only when all three SIZA checks are complete.
- Do not claim Home Affairs verification, criminal-record checks, insurance, guarantees, reviews, secure payment, escrow or payment protection.
- Do not describe a cleaner as approved unless the admin approval checklist is complete.
- Do not present placeholder imagery as SIZA cleaners or completed SIZA work.
- Keep the payment disclosure exact: “At launch, payment is arranged directly between you and the cleaner after the job. SIZA does not hold funds or cover payment disputes.”
- Keep the audience broad enough for homes, short stays and offices; cleaner recruitment is the primary hero action.
