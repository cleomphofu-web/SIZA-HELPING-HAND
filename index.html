Safety & trust

Answering the real questions

You are letting someone into your home or guest accommodation. Here is how we protect your space.

100% ID-Vetted & Residential References

Every helper must provide a valid 13-digit South African ID document, verified phone number, and a verifiable past employer reference before receiving access to job requests.

4-Digit Arrival Check-In Code

Helpers cannot claim arrival remotely. A unique 4-digit security code is generated on your screen. You provide it only when they physically arrive at your property.

One-Tap Incident & Report Button

If a helper is late, non-communicative, or unprofessional, one tap on your request page logs an emergency report directly with our Johannesburg operations team.

Damage & Breakage Protocol

In the rare event of accidental damage or missing property, we provide full verified helper identity records, timestamped check-in logs, and liaison for immediate insurance and police reports.
